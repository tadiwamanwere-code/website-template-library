Approach
§ 01 — 04
Fig. 01 — Studio floor, Ritterstraße, Kreuzberg

Monolith is one director and a bench. I write the strategy, art-direct the work, and stay on the file until the last spec sheet is signed off. Specialists — a typographer in Leipzig, a motion lead in Lisbon, a fabricator in Rotterdam — come in per project, never on retainer.

The output is a system with reasons attached. Every decision ships with the argument behind it, so the team inheriting it can extend the work without calling me. That is the entire point: identity that survives its author.

Engagements run six to fourteen weeks. I take four a year. If the brief needs sixty people, I will tell you who to call instead.

  1. 01 / DiagnoseTwo weeks inside the business. Interviews, shelf audit, the numbers nobody circulates.
  2. 02 / PositionOne sentence the CEO and the warehouse both recognise as true.
  3. 03 / SystemType, grid, colour, motion, tone — built as rules with stated reasons.
  4. 04 / EvidenceApplied to the twelve surfaces that actually decide whether it works.

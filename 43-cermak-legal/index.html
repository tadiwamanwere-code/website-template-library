Why Choose Us

The Firm represents a wide range of industrial and manufacturing clients in environmental matters throughout the United States, focusing on administrative and judicial litigation, complex contamination sites, compliance counseling, the environmental aspects of real estate and corporate transactions, and OSHA inspections and enforcement. Our ability to view complex environmental law matters from our clients’ perspective enables us to collaborate with them to craft practical and durable solutions. Using good judgment informed by decades of experience, we focus on developing creative, cost-effective solutions that place our clients in favorable positions.

  • Experience in handling complex environmental matters.
  • Creative, strategic and practical advice and solutions.
  • Environmental lawyers with large-firm and government experience.
  • Experience representing clients in the intersection of environmental law and other legal disciplines.
  • Experienced across all environmental disciplines, including counseling, transactions and litigation.

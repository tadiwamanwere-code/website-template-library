§ 01

Position

Method / Materials / Duty of care

We do not begin with form. We begin with what is already standing — its span, its bearing capacity, the direction its water runs — and we argue with it until a building appears.

Every commission opens with a structural survey and a carbon budget agreed before a single plan is drawn. Since 2016 the practice has declined all work requiring demolition of a sound primary structure. Eleven of our last fourteen projects are adaptive reuse.

Survey before sketch
Six weeks of measured survey precede the first drawing. We record the building as found — settlement, patch repairs, all of it — and publish that record to the client before design begins.

One material, honestly
Board-formed concrete, lime-mortared brick, hot-rolled steel, oiled Douglas fir. No cladding that conceals what carries the load. Joints are drawn at 1:5 before the elevation is fixed.
2012

Carbon as a client

In detail — Silo Vier

Kattendijkdok, Antwerp — 2018 / 2022
ProjectSilo Vier
Cells cored42 of 60
Module3.2 m
Concrete reused on site4,120 t
The problem

Sixty grain cells, 42 metres tall, 6.4 metres across, with 280 mm slip-formed walls and no openings above level two. Demolition was consented and funded when we were appointed.

The move

Rather than perforate every cell, we cored 42 of them from the crown down on a single module and left 18 intact as the lateral bracing system — turning the silo's own stiffness into the structure that makes the openings possible. The market floor sits in the void of four merged cells at grade.

The result

96 apartments, a 1,400 m² covered market and a public roof terrace at 44 metres, delivered for 71% of the tendered demolition-and-rebuild cost. The crushed concrete from coring returned to the building as aggregate in all new floor plates — 4,120 tonnes that never left the quay. Awarded the Belgian Concrete Prize in 2023 and shortlisted for the EU Mies Award the following cycle.

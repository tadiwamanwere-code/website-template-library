# 21 — Mirage International College (education / vocational training)

- **Inspiration:** Mirage International College, Harare (`mirage-college.vercel.app`) — an ITEC/VTCT accredited beauty-therapy school that teaches above a working spa. Screenshot of the original: `_inspo-notes/rylo-originals/mirage.png`.
- **Steal this:** the **pinned course browser**. One viewport is held while the scroll drives a five-step index; the course panel cross-dissolves and its photograph scales back from 1.13 as the detail lines stagger in. Snapping (`snapTo: 1/(n-1)`) makes it settle on a course rather than drift between two. The index is clickable and maps a course number straight back to a scroll position inside the pinned range, so it reads as a browser, not a slideshow.
- **Also:** hand-split line-mask reveals on every serif heading (words wrapped, grouped by `offsetTop`, each line in an `overflow:hidden` mask, `yPercent 112 → 0`), count-ups on the four outcome figures, `clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0)` reveals with a counter-scale on all gallery and spa imagery, multi-speed parallax on hero/CTA plates, a lift-away monogram curtain on load, and magnetic gold CTAs.
- **Accent:** `#BFA05A` (warm gold), on a deep olive ground `#1A2117` and warm paper `#F6F2E9`.
- **Type:** Playfair Display (high-contrast display serif, 500) + Source Sans 3 (humanist sans for body, labels and UI). New pairing for the library — no other template uses either face.
- **Structural differentiator vs. the other "shared house pattern" rebuilds (Gordon's, Safeway, Smile Dentist):** this one is **course-led**. Below the fold the page is organised around the enrolment decision — five costed courses, the working-spa proposition, outcomes, open day, then a fees-and-payment-plans table — rather than around services or rooms.

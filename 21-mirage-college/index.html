The proposition

Your hands are on a real client in week five.

Mirage Spa on Fifth Street is not a practice room with a plastic couch in it. It is a working spa with six treatment rooms, a price list, a booking diary and around forty paying clients a week. The college sits above it.

Students move onto the floor in week five, supervised, on discounted treatments the public books like any other. They learn what a difficult back feels like, how to hold a consultation, how to keep a room turning over, and what to say when a client is unhappy. None of that can be taught on a mannequin.

  1. Week 01–04

    The practical room

    Theory, anatomy and technique on each other. Nobody touches a paying client until the tutor signs the sheet.

  2. Week 05

    First supervised client

    One treatment, one tutor in the room, one written consultation. The client pays a student rate and knows exactly who they are booking.

  3. Week 12

    The full list

    Facials, waxing, nails and brows across two rooms. You start being booked by name, and repeat clients begin to build.

  4. Week 30

    Your own column

    A half-day column in the diary, run start to finish: greeting, consultation, treatment, retail, rebooking, room turnaround.

A Level 2 graduate leaves with 120 supervised client hours. A Level 3 graduate leaves with 240. Six rooms, six days a week, and every hour countersigned.

Enrolling, in four steps

  1. 01

    Enquire

    WhatsApp or email the registrar. Tell us which course and which intake. You will get the full prospectus and the syllabus as a PDF the same day.

  2. 02

    Visit, or call

    Twenty minutes with a tutor, in person or on the phone. We look at your schooling, your hours and whether Level 2 or a short course is the right start.

  3. 03

    Place your deposit

    US$400 on the diplomas, US$150–200 on short courses. It holds one of the twelve places and comes off the fee. Refundable up to two weeks before the intake.

  4. 04

    Kit fitting and induction

    The Friday before term. Uniform fitted, kit issued and signed for, spa floor rules, and your first timetable.

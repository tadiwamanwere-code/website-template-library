The firm

A practice built
on a clean ledger

Fourteen years in Kadoma, working with millers, hardware groups, clinics, schools, contractors and co-operatives across Mashonaland West. Most clients arrive behind. Nobody stays behind.

We are not a filing service. A return is the last five minutes of a month of work, and if the month was done properly the return takes care of itself.

That means the bank, the cash box and the mobile money wallet reconcile every month, not every March. It means the payroll and the PAYE schedule agree before the P2 goes out. It means the trial balance is built from documents you can produce if ZIMRA asks for them, because sooner or later ZIMRA asks for them.

Where records have fallen behind we rebuild them from bank statements, EcoCash exports and supplier invoices, file what is outstanding, and deal with the penalty position directly with the revenue authority rather than leaving it hanging over you.

You get a named accountant, a fixed monthly fee agreed in advance, and a management pack on the same day each month.

01 — Bookkeeping

Records kept
current

The day-to-day book, done monthly, so the ledger always reflects what the business actually did rather than what someone remembers it doing.

  • Income and expenditure captured from source documents
  • Bank, petty cash, EcoCash and OneMoney reconciliations
  • Debtors and creditors ledgers with an ageing report
  • Arrears rebuild for books that have fallen behind

Who it’s for Any trading business with more than a handful of transactions a week, and anyone whose last set of books stopped mid-year.

02 — Accounting

Trial balance
to year end

Your books turned into a complete, balanced set of accounts that a bank, a board or an auditor will accept without a covering letter.

  • General ledger, trial balance and journal control
  • Month-end and year-end close with a signed working file
  • Annual financial statements under IFRS for SMEs
  • Correction and formal restatement of prior periods

Who it’s for Private companies, PBCs and trusts that need statements for lenders, tenders, shareholders or a statutory audit.

03 — Tax & ZIMRA

Every return,
on the date

Registration, filing and correspondence handled end to end, with every legitimate deduction claimed and every acknowledgement kept on file.

  • VAT7 returns, Category A, B and C
  • ITF12C self-assessment and QPD provisional payments
  • ITF263 tax clearance and BP number registration
  • Audit queries, objections and payment-plan negotiation

Who it’s for VAT-registered traders, companies on quarterly QPDs, and anyone holding a ZIMRA letter they have not opened yet.

04 — Payroll

Paid right,
paid on time

A full payroll run with payslips issued, statutory deductions calculated, and the schedules that go with them submitted before the deadline.

  • Monthly runs and individual payslips, up to 120 staff
  • PAYE (P2), NSSA, ZIMDEF and standards levy computation
  • Leave, bonus, gratuity and terminal benefit calculations
  • ITF16 annual reconciliation and payroll audit file

Who it’s for Employers of five staff and up, especially anyone still running the payroll on a spreadsheet in the back office.

05 — Reporting

Numbers you
can act on

A management pack on the same day each month showing where the money went, what it earned, and how far that sits from what you planned.

  • Monthly management accounts, delivered by the 12th
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet and 13-week cash flow
  • Budget versus actual with variance commentary
  • Lender, board and donor reporting packs

Who it’s for Owner-managers running more than one revenue line, and any business reporting to a funder, a bank or a board.

06 — Advisory

The decision
behind it

Costing, structure and planning work. The part where the accounts stop being a compliance chore and start telling you what to do next.

  • Company formation: CR6, CR14 and incorporation
  • Costing, pricing and gross-margin review by product line
  • Budgeting, cash-flow forecasting and funding applications
  • Structure, systems and internal-control design

Who it’s for Businesses about to grow, formalise, take on debt, or split a partnership into something with a share register.

What it was worth

Rimuka Milling (Pvt) Ltd · Kadoma · 2024

Thirty-one months
of unfiled VAT

A maize and stockfeed miller arrived with a ZIMRA assessment, no VAT returns since 2021, and a bookkeeper who had left the country. Records existed only as bank statements and a shoebox of supplier invoices.

We rebuilt thirty-one periods from source, filed every outstanding VAT7 and ITF12C, and took the penalty position to ZIMRA with a full reconciliation attached. The assessed exposure of US$41,200 settled at US$6,850 on a six-month payment plan.

Patel Hardware Group · Chegutu & Kadoma

Payroll off the spreadsheet

Seventy-eight staff across four branches were being paid from a workbook that nobody had rebuilt since 2019. Annual PAYE variance of US$3,140 went to zero in the first quarter, and the monthly run dropped from three days to four hours. ITF16 reconciliation now closes without a query.

Sanyati Cotton Collective · Mashonaland West

First statements, first facility

A 214-member growers’ co-operative had never produced statements a bank would read. We built two comparative years under IFRS for SMEs, prepared the audit file and sat in the credit meeting. Result: a US$120,000 input facility approved, at 4.2% below the rate first quoted.

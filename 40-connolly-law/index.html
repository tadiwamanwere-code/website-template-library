Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if my truck is found overweight?
The driver or commercial carrier could receive citations and be fined thousands of dollars, or even tens of thousands of dollars.
Do I need an attorney for an overweight truck violation?
An attorney with experience in overweight truck violations can challenge the validity of citations and often negotiate substantial reductions in fines that could otherwise threaten a trucking company’s financial well-being.
I’ve been charged with DUI. Could that affect my professional license?
A DUI conviction can have significantly negative effects on a person’s personal, family, and professional life. For truck drivers, a DUI conviction can result in the loss of a commercial driver’s license (CDL), immediately affecting their ability to work.
How does a real estate lawyer help when buying property?
Buying a home or commercial property is a big investment. A real estate attorney reviews the contract and proposes modifications, reviews the title for possible liens, and negotiates proper tax credits.

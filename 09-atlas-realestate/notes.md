# 09 — Campo Alto (residential development, Lisboa)

- **Inspiration:** premium property brochures; warm, calm, residential — deliberately not stark or fashion-loud.
- **Steal this:** the **sticky stacking residence cards** — each card pins via `position: sticky` and scales/fades down as the next slides over it, giving a physical deck-of-cards feel driven entirely by scroll.
- **Also:** count-up on units/floors/sqft, clip-path reveals, offset editorial image-text pairs with imagery bleeding off one edge.
- **Accent:** `#A0563A` (terracotta) on sand/bone.
- **Type:** Newsreader (transitional serif) + Archivo (UI/data).

02 — The journey

Measure. Design.
Manufacture. Install.

Four steps, four dates in your diary, and one company answering for all of them. Here is exactly what happens, who does it, and how long it takes.

Step 01 / 04 01

Measure

60–90 minutes

A cabinetmaker comes to the room, not a salesman. We laser-measure wall to wall at three heights, find how far out of square the room actually is, mark every service — water, waste, gas, sockets, the geyser pipe nobody mentioned — and photograph the whole run. You get the survey, whether or not you order from us.

Your site
Who comes

Working tolerance: ±2 mm across a 6 metre run.

Step 02 / 04 02

Design

3–5 working days

We draw the run in 3D, then price it line by line so you can see what every choice costs before you make it. Swap quartz for laminate and watch the number move. Two revisions are included. Nothing reaches the workshop until you have signed a drawing with real dimensions on it, not a mood board.

Where
Greendale studio
Revisions

You get
3D views, elevations, itemised quote
Deposit

Step 03 / 04 03

Manufacture

12–18 working days

Boards are cut on our beam saw, edged on a PUR bander for a joint you cannot feel with a fingernail, and drilled on the CNC to the 32 mm system so shelves land where the drawing says. Sprayed fronts go through a dust-extracted booth: primer, denib, two coats, cure. Every carcass is dry-assembled and numbered before it leaves the floor.

Cutting, edging, drilling, spraying and assembly all happen under one roof.

Step 04 / 04 04

Install

1–3 days

Our own fitters, never a subcontractor. Floors get covered before a single unit comes through the door. Units are scribed to the wall so there is no filler strip, doors are set to a 3 mm shadow gap across the run, worktops are templated and fitted, and the offcuts leave with us. You walk the job, we close the snag list, you sign the handover.

FIRE PROTECTION

The only approved our fire treatment
applicator in your country.

Only approved applicator in your country
Certified via Micon Coatings, RSA

Thatch is beautiful, cool and quiet. Its one real risk is fire. our fire treatment removes that risk without changing how a roof looks, smells or breathes.

How it works

On exposure to heat the coating forms an insulating char across the surface of the thatch. That char limits heat flow and starves the grass of the oxygen a fire needs to spread.

Whatever starts it

Electrical short circuit, lightning strike, a spark off a boma fire, or a neighbour’s bush fire. our fire treatment does not care what the ignition source was.

It still looks like thatch

The application does not affect the visual appearance of the structure, and leaves no unpleasant smell in the serviced area. Guests will never know it is there.

Applied under certification

our fire treatment may only be applied by an approved applicator using specialised equipment. YourBusiness3 is certified through Micon Coatings of South Africa, and is the only approved applicator in your country.

Illustrative photo

From a risk and security perspective this makes YourBusiness3 a natural partner for insurers underwriting thatched property. The application is a must-have for individuals, companies and insurance firms alike.

For lodges, hotels and insurers

We will survey the thatch, report on its condition and fire risk, and price the our fire treatment application — in writing, in a form your insurer can use.

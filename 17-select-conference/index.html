A conference sized to the room, not to the sponsor deck.

OFFSET is a one-day conference in Copenhagen for engineers working below the product line — storage, runtimes, compilers, platform, the parts of the stack that only get attention when they break. It started in 2024 as 90 people in a rented cinema on Gothersgade. It is now 480 people in a converted cattle hall, and the format has not changed: one room, one track, everybody sees the same talks.

We do not sell keynote slots. Every speaker is invited because they shipped the thing they are talking about, and every talk has to include at least one part that went badly. Q&A is moderated and runs long. Lunch is included and eaten standing up, on purpose, because the hallway track is the actual product.

Tickets are flat-priced. There is no VIP tier, no early-bird trickery beyond the first release, and student tickets are subsidised out of the general pool — 40 of them, first come.

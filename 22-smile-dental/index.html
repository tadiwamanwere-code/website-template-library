Our approach

We take the fear out
of it, then we take
the work seriously.

Most people who walk through our door have put this off. Sometimes for a year, sometimes for a decade. Nobody at Linden will make you feel bad about that, and nobody will hand you a treatment plan on a first visit you did not ask for.

What we will do is look properly, show you the photographs on the screen, tell you what is urgent and what can safely be watched, and give you a written price for every stage before a single instrument comes out.

  1. 01Nothing starts without a quoteEvery plan is priced per stage, in writing, before you agree to it. If something changes mid-treatment we stop and ask.
  2. 02A tooth we can watch, we watchEarly decay gets fluoride and a six-month recall, not a drill. We photograph it so you can see for yourself next time.
  3. 03You can stop at any pointRaise a hand and everything stops. It is written into how we work, and we say it out loud before we begin.
  4. 04The person who examines you treats youNo hand-offs between clinicians halfway through a plan unless you ask for a second opinion.

Prices are the honest starting figure in US dollars, not a teaser. Medical aid is claimed directly and you settle the shortfall only. Anything above $400 can be spread over three months, interest free.

Before you commit

A first examination and X-rays is $45. If you decide to go no further, that is where it ends. We will still send you the photographs and the written plan.

A full check of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues, two small digital X-rays if they are due, then a scale, polish and air-flow stain removal with the hygienist. You leave with intra-oral photographs, a written plan, and a price for anything we found.

PreventiveDigital X-rayAir-flow polish

A gentle look, a ride in the chair, fluoride varnish, and fissure sealants on the back teeth once the adult molars are through. On a first visit we will not put an instrument near a child who would rather just sit and watch. Free for under-fives booked alongside a parent’s check-up.

PreventiveFluoride varnishFree under 5

Composite bonded under rubber dam and shade-matched to the tooth beside it, so the repair disappears. Topical numbing gel goes on three minutes before the injection, which is why most patients tell us they never felt it. Guaranteed for two years.

RestorativeRubber dam2-year guarantee

Rotary files under magnification, an apex locator rather than guesswork, and a rubber dam throughout. Most single-canal front teeth are finished in one sitting; molars usually take two. A crown afterwards is normally the sensible next step and we will price it up front.

RestorativeSedation availableMagnification

Trays scanned and printed here, ten per cent carbamide peroxide worn overnight for two weeks, then one in-surgery session to set the final shade. Desensitising gel is included, and so is a top-up syringe twelve months later. We photograph the starting shade so the change is measured, not imagined.

CosmeticCustom traysTop-up included

A digital scan, then a printed model of the finished bite that you hold in your hand before you commit to anything. Aligners change weekly, reviews take five minutes every six weeks, and retainers plus one round of refinements are in the price. Payable over the length of treatment.

CosmeticPrinted previewRetainers included

Also at Linden — single-tooth implants from $1,150, wisdom-tooth removal from $180, night guards from $140, and same-day emergency relief for registered patients. Ask at reception, or mention it in the notes when you book.

Your first visit

Step 01

Arrive

Fifteen minutes early is plenty. There is tea, there is water, and there is no clove smell in the waiting room, because we use citrus. Reception will already have your name.

Step 02

Talk

Ten minutes, sitting upright, fully clothed, chair still upright. What has been bothering you, what you are worried about, what you would rather not have done, and what you would like to spend.

Step 03

Look

Only then does the chair go back. Intra-oral camera, small digital X-rays if they are needed, and everything up on the screen beside you so that you are looking at exactly what we are looking at.

Step 04

Plan

You leave with photographs, a written plan in stages, and a price for each stage. Nothing is booked until you say so. Most people go home and think about it, and that is entirely fine.

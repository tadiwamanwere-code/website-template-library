01 — The Firm

Based in Harare

Building Work That Lasts

We are contractors, not consultants. Every job is priced by the people who will build it, run by a permanent site agent, and handed over with a full as-built set.

We started small, with a few machines and a willingness to take the jobs other firms turned down. We still run our own plant, we still employ our crews directly, and we still price every job ourselves. That is the whole method, and it is why clients come back.

Concrete, roads, water, steel. We work in-house from setting out to commissioning, we do not sub-let the structural frame, and the person who prices your job is the person who runs it.

05 — Method

From Consultation to Handover

Six stages  /  one site agent
  1. 01

    Consultation
    & Brief

    We meet on site, not in a boardroom. You tell us what has to be built, by when, and what it has to cost. We tell you straight away whether that is possible.

    Typically 1 week
  2. 02

    Survey
    & Feasibility

    Topographical survey, trial pits and a geotechnical report. If the ground will not carry it you find out now, not at foundation stage.

    2–3 weeks
  3. 03

    Design
    & Costing

    Structural drawings coordinated with services, then a full bill of quantities priced line by line by the contracts manager who will run the job.

    3–4 weeks
  4. 04

    Mobilisation

    Site establishment, hoarding, temporary works, plant delivery and the safety file. Nothing breaks ground until that file is signed off.

    1–2 weeks
  5. 05

    On Site

    Permanent site agent, weekly valuations, monthly progress photographs and a programme reissued every Friday whether the news is good or not.

    Programme dependent
  6. 06

    Commissioning
    & Handover

    Snag, test, commission, then hand over the as-builts and the O&M manuals. Twelve-month defects liability period, honoured without argument.

    12-month DLP

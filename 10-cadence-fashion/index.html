THE COLLECTION

Twenty-four looks. Eleven materials. One rule — every seam is cut through, then re-joined by hand so the join is the ornament.

ATELIER

01 — POSITION

Cut it open.

Show the join.

METHOD

Panels are severed along the grain, offset four millimetres, then bridged with a flat felled seam in contrast thread. Nothing is hidden in the lining.

PRODUCTION

Cut runs capped at 120 units per style. Outerwear is numbered on the inside placket; every jacket carries the cutter's initials.

FIT

Graded 34–46 IT on a 6 cm drop. Tailoring runs one size generous through the shoulder — take your true size for the intended line.

03 — DESIGNER'S NOTE

Marit Halvorsen
Founder & Creative Director
Antwerp, March 2026

Severance began with a coat I ruined. I cut into a wool officer coat that had taken nine days to sample, straight down the back panel, because the line was too polite. The repair — four millimetres of offset, a flat felled seam in a thread two tones off — was the only honest thing in the room.

So the collection took that as its rule. Every garment here is severed and re-joined. Not distressed, not deconstructed — those words have gone soft. Severed: a clean cut, made deliberately, then closed by a hand that leaves evidence. The join is the ornament. There is no other decoration in twenty-four looks.

What surprised us was the weight. Once you accept the seam as the subject, you stop needing volume to make a silhouette. The greatcoat lost 400 grams. The cape lost its lining entirely. Antwerp winters will test that decision, and I am content to be tested.

04 — SOURCED FOR FW26. MILL, WEIGHT AND CERTIFICATION ON REQUEST.

M/01

Pressed Wool

Vitale Barberis Canonico, Biella. 780 gsm, milled four passes then hot-pressed flat so the cut edge holds without fusing. Used in looks 01, 09, 17.

M/02

Silk Charmeuse

Taroni, Como. 19 momme, sand-washed once. Cut on the true bias and left raw at the hem — it will fray six to eight millimetres and then stop.

M/03

Double-Faced Cashmere

Todd & Duncan yarn, woven in Scotland. Two faces separated by hand across 3.1 metres per cape, then closed with a saddle stitch. Forty pieces only.

M/04

Oiled Calf

Tärnsjö Garveri, Sweden. Vegetable-tanned, 1.2 mm, hot-stuffed. Gold-rated LWG tannery; hides traced to four farms within 300 km of the works.

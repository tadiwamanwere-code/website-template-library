# 10 — Cadence, "Severance" FW26 (fashion lookbook)

- **Inspiration:** magazine mastheads and editorial lookbooks; the most graphically severe template in the library — pure black/white, one stark red.
- **Steal this:** **scroll-velocity skew** — Lenis scroll velocity is mapped onto a slight `skewY` on the imagery, so the whole page subtly deforms with momentum. Paired with a contextual custom cursor that grows into a "VIEW" disc over looks.
- **Also:** pinned + horizontal lookbook track, character mask reveals on the huge condensed type, clip reveals.
- **Accent:** `#FF1E00`, used once. Otherwise pure monochrome.
- **Type:** Anton (condensed display) + Archivo (micro-labels). Extreme scale contrast is the point.

# 15 — Solstice Alaló (private island resort)

- **Inspiration:** high-end travel and hospitality; full-bleed imagery with generous, unhurried pacing.
- **Steal this:** **multi-depth parallax chapters** — foreground copy, mid-plate imagery and background plates each move at different scroll speeds, so moving through the page feels like moving through physical space.
- **Also:** sticky stacking chapter cards, magnetic enquiry button, clip-path reveals, count-up on villa/acreage figures.
- **Accent:** `#B8763F` (warm bronze).
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display serif) + Jost (UI). Shares its serif with 07 but reads entirely differently — warm and light rather than near-black.

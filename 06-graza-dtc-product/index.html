Why It Slaps

Not another vinegar bomb.

Most hot sauces are 70% vinegar wearing a chili costume. Hotshot skips the filler entirely — we cold-press whole Fresno chilis and key limes the same week they're picked, so what you get is bright, fruity heat that actually tastes like the pepper it came from. No xanthan gum, no "natural flavor" mystery box, no burn-and-forget. Just squeeze, taste, and go back for more.

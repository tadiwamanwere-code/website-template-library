Four rooms

Every room is different. That is the point.

We stopped at four. Each one was worked out around the light it gets and the shape of the wall it sits behind, so no two are alike. Rates include breakfast and are the same whether you book direct or not — we would just rather you booked direct.

The house

Ruth and Samson took it on in 2009.

Ruth grew up two streets away and had walked past the house for thirty years before it came up for sale. Samson cooks. They live on the ground floor at the back, which is why somebody is always in when you arrive.

The long table. Breakfast runs 7:30 to 9:30, and later if you ask the night before.

Who runs it

Two people and, at weekends, their daughter Ada. There is no front desk and no night porter. If the bell goes at eleven, one of them will still answer it — but do call first, so somebody is awake and expecting you.

What breakfast is like

One long table, everybody at it. Bread out of the oven at seven, eggs from four hens named Dot, Marge, Pin and one nobody got round to naming, figs from the courtyard in season, and coffee ground when you sit down. Porridge if you would rather. In your room if you say so the night before.

What the mornings are like

Quiet until eight. Nobody knocks on a door before then, housekeeping starts at half ten, and the kettle in the hall is on from six for anybody who wakes early. Most guests read on the verandah for an hour before they decide what to do with the day.

Within a walk

Everything worth doing is close enough to walk to.

Chelmer Vale is small. You will not need a car once you have parked, and the six things below are the six we actually send people to.

Loveridge Bakery

Sourdough out at seven, sold out by eleven most days. Take the cash, they have never fixed the card machine.

4 min walk

The Saturday market, Fenner Street

Vegetables, three cheesemongers, and a man who sells only honey and will talk to you about it for an hour.

6 min walk

The reservoir path

A flat 5.2 km loop on gravel, doable in an hour. Herons at the north end, early.

9 min walk

The Crown & Anchor

A proper pub with a proper kitchen. Book a table on Fridays or you will be eating at the bar.

11 min walk

Chelmer Hill lookout

Twenty minutes up through the beeches and the whole valley opens underneath you. Best an hour before sunset.

22 min walk

Bus 14 to the mainline station

Every twenty minutes until 22:40. Eighteen minutes end to end, and it stops at the top of our road.

3 min walk

Walking times measured from the front gate, at an unhurried pace, by Samson, twice.

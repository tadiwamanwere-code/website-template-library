# Gordon's Bnb — generation prompt

**Category:** Hospitality · Travel
**Best for:** guesthouses, boutique hotels, lodges, self-catering, retreats, small B&Bs
**Signature motion:** a pinned full-bleed crossfade image sequence that holds each frame for a whole screen-height of scroll, with a slow ken-burns drift underneath

---

Build a single self-contained `index.html` (inline `<style>` and `<script>`, GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger + Lenis via CDN, Google Fonts via `<link>`, no build step, no React) for **[PROPERTY NAME]**, a [ROOM COUNT]-room guesthouse in **[LOCATION]**. The tone is calm, warm, personal and a little old-fashioned in a good way. It should feel like a place you would sleep well in.

**This is a restrained page. Restraint beats motion here.** Long durations, gentle easing, few techniques. Nothing snaps, bounces or races. If an effect would make the page feel busy, choose a quieter one or leave it out.

**Palette.** Warm oat paper `#F2EBDF`, secondary paper `#EAE1D1`, warm near-black ink `#221C14`, muted ink `#6E624F`, dark section ground `#17120C`, cream `#F6F1E7`. One accent only: warm gold `[ACCENT #B8873F]`, used on the pill CTA, the eyebrow rules and the sequence progress bar — never as a background wash. Darken the accent to about `#8C6027` for small uppercase type on the cream ground so it still reads. No decorative gradients: the only gradients allowed are dark-to-transparent legibility scrims over photos. No coloured icon chips, no emoji bullets.

**Type.** Playfair Display (display serif, 400/500 + italic) for every heading, room name, pull quote and review; DM Sans (300/400/500) for nav, labels, body and all figures, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on every number. Hero headline `clamp(46px, 8.6vw, 150px)`, line-height `.94`, letter-spacing `-.028em`. Uppercase eyebrows at 11px with `letter-spacing: .22em` and a short leading rule.

**Sections, in order.**

1. **Load intro.** A cream curtain holding a circular line-drawn monogram, the wordmark and "[LOCATION] · est. [YEAR]". It lifts with `clipPath: inset(0% 0% 100% 0%)`, 1.15s `power3.inOut`, while the hero photo settles from `scale 1.09 → 1` over 2.6s. Total about 3.3s. Not a body fade.
2. **Hero.** Full-bleed guesthouse photo under a warm multiply wash plus a dark scrim. Bottom-left: eyebrow "[EYEBROW, e.g. BOUTIQUE BED & BREAKFAST]", the headline **"[HEADLINE, e.g. A quiet retreat, beautifully kept.]"** on two hand-written mask lines, one paragraph, a gold pill "Check availability" (dark ink text on the gold, not white) and an outline pill "Explore the rooms →", then the price line "Rooms from **[NIGHTLY RATE]** per night". Fixed nav above it: circular monogram + wordmark left, links centre, gold pill right; it swaps to a solid paper bar past the hero.
3. **The signature sequence — "One morning at [PROPERTY NAME]".** Dark section. A short heading, then a `100svh` stage pinned with ScrollTrigger (`scrub: 1.1`) holding 5 full-bleed frames. Give each frame roughly one viewport-height of scroll: about two thirds hold, one third crossfade (`0.38` units, `power1.inOut`). Under each frame run a `scale 1 → 1.075` drift. Each frame carries a timestamp in the accent (`[06:40]`, `[07:10]`, …) and one line of serif copy about that moment. Bottom right: a hairline progress rail and an `01 / 05` counter.
4. **The rooms — the biggest section and the reason people book.** Four full-width editorial rows, photo and text alternating sides, tall `4/5` photos with a small printed index tab. Each room gets a name in Playfair, a paragraph saying **what makes it different** (be specific and physical — the light it gets, the bath under the skylight, no stairs), then a hairline spec list: **Size** `[SIZE m²]`, **Bed** `[BED CONFIG] · sleeps [N]`, **View** `[VIEW]`, **Rate** `[NIGHTLY RATE] per night, breakfast included`. End each row with a "Check dates for this room" text link that sets the booking form and scrolls to it.
5. **Booking block.** Dark section, a bordered two-panel grid. Left: room select, check-in and check-out `<input type="date">` (with `color-scheme: dark`), guests select, plus soft inline hints for minimum stay and room capacity. Right: room name, the date range in words, line items (`[RATE] × N nights`, breakfast included, one-off housekeeping `[FEE]`) and a large live total. **Client-side only, no backend.** The total counts up over 0.95s `power2.out` the first time the block is seen and on every change. Seed the dates two weeks out.
6. **The house and its story.** Cream. Who runs it ([OWNER NAMES], since [YEAR]), what breakfast is like (times, the bread, the table), what the mornings are like. Three hairline-topped columns, an offset photo with a caption, a large italic serif pull quote attributed to the owner, and a four-up row of plain facts.
7. **The area.** Six genuinely useful things nearby as hairline rows: name in serif, one honest sentence, and a **walking time** in tabular figures. Row hover warms the background and grows a short accent rule beside the time.
8. **Guest reviews.** Four quotes in italic serif with real names, the room they stayed in, and the stay month. Small round greyscale portraits. An average score that counts up beside the heading.
9. **House notes.** A two-column spec table with hover states: check-in `[CHECK-IN TIME]`, check-out `[CHECK-OUT TIME]`, children, pets, parking, cancellation, payment, smoking. Write them as a person would say them.
10. **Closing CTA** over a dusk photo, then a four-column footer with real link groups, address, phone and email.

**Motion — exactly these four, all gentle.** (1) the pinned crossfade sequence; (2) line-mask reveals on the serif headings — split text into lines yourself by measuring `offsetTop`, wrap each in `overflow:hidden`, `yPercent: 118 → 0`, `duration: 1.45`, `power3.out`, `stagger: 0.09`; (3) parallax on the room photography at `yPercent: -4 → 4` with `scrub: 1.2`, nothing larger; (4) the booking total count-up. Wire Lenis with `duration: 1.45` (slower than usual, on purpose), `lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)` and a `gsap.ticker` raf loop. **Do not add** marquees, magnetic buttons, custom cursors, velocity skew, horizontal tracks, or `back` / `elastic` easing.

**Hard rules.** Copy must be specific and invented for this property — named rooms, named hens, real walking times, an actual cancellation window. Never lorem ipsum or generic filler. Animate only `transform` and `opacity`. Include a `prefers-reduced-motion` block that kills the curtain, the pin and every scrub and shows the final state. Below 1024px drop the pin and let the sequence stack vertically; drop parallax below 900px.

LedgerCash

Positioning & forecasting

Direct API and ISO 20022 connections to 214 banks in 38 markets, normalised into one entity graph. Balances refresh on webhook, not on a nightly MT940 batch, so the 09:00 position is the position — including the accounts your subsidiaries opened without telling you.

Rolling 13-week forecasts reconcile actuals against plan daily and attribute variance to entity, counterparty and currency. Median forecast error at week 4 across the client base is 3.8%.

02 / FX

LedgerFX

Exposure & hedging

Exposure is written at booking. Every AR and AP document raised in your ERP creates a dated FX position in the same second, so the hedge programme runs against transactions rather than against a month-end snapshot of them.

Policy is expressed as code — ratio bands, tenor limits, counterparty caps — and enforced pre-trade. Execution routes to seven liquidity providers with full RFQ records retained for seven years against IFRS 9 hedge documentation.

Settlement & orchestration

We hold the licences and the scheme memberships — FPS and CHAPS in the UK, SEPA Inst in the euro area, FedNow and RTP in the US, FAST in Singapore — so a payment is settled by us, not handed to a correspondent and tracked by inference.

Intercompany nets before it moves: 4,118 gross transfers collapsed to 372 external settlements last quarter for one client, removing $1.9m of annual wire and FX spread cost.

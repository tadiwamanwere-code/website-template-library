# 07 — Kuroba (fine-dining omakase)

- **Inspiration:** dark-luxury restaurant sites; the serif-over-photo register of godaylight.com's hero, pushed further into near-black.
- **Steal this:** the **pinned tasting-menu scrub** (`pin: '#menuStage'`, `scrub: 0.7`) — the section holds the viewport while the six courses advance and plate imagery cross-dissolves, with an index rail whose buttons scrub the same timeline. A second pinned gallery reuses the pattern.
- **Also:** clip-path image reveals, count-up figures, magnetic "Reserve" button.
- **Accent:** `#A32B1E` (lacquer red) on near-black `#0b0b0c`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display serif) + JetBrains Mono (labels).

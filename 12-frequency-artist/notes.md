# 12 — Odessa Voss, "Blackout Choir" (recording artist)

- **Inspiration:** record-label and artist sites; loud display type over near-black, one acid accent.
- **Steal this:** the **canvas visualiser** — a reactive waveform/frequency field drawn in `<canvas>` and driven by an animation loop, sitting behind the type as living album art rather than a static image.
- **Also:** scroll-velocity skew, magnetic buttons, clip reveals, count-up on release/tour figures.
- **Accent:** `#c8ff3d` (acid yellow-green) on near-black.
- **Type:** Archivo Black (display) + JetBrains Mono (metadata).

SESSIONS

Recorded in a dead telephone exchange

Odessa Voss spent eleven weeks of winter 2025 in Fold Studios, Lisbon — a switching hall that carried the city's calls until 1998 and has kept the acoustics ever since. Nothing was re-amped. The room is on every take.

Co-producer Ilya Brandt ran the band through a pair of failing tape machines, printing each pass twice and letting the drift between them become the record's tuning.

FIELD NOTE — 14.01.26

“We stopped fixing the wow and flutter around track five. It was doing something a click track never does — it made the choir sound like it was remembering the song instead of singing it.”

Nine voices from the Alfama community choir appear across four tracks, recorded in a single Sunday afternoon with two ribbon mics at the far end of the hall.

“Voss builds cathedrals out of tape hiss — and then turns the lights off.”
Slow Static, issue 34
